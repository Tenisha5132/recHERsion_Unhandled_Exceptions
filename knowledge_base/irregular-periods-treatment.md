Treatment for irregular periods isn't always needed. If treatment is required, it depends on the cause. For example, if polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS/PCOS) is causing irregular periods, the combined contraceptive pill may help make periods more regular. If irregular periods are caused by other hormone problems like thyroid issues, medicines can help treat the underlying cause. A GP may refer to a specialist if tests are needed to find out what's causing irregular periods.
