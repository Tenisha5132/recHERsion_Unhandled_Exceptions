When seeing a GP about heavy periods, they will usually ask about symptoms, how they affect daily life, and medical history. They may ask about how often pads or tampons are changed, period length, clots, and any other symptoms. A physical examination may be offered, and blood tests may be done to check for iron deficiency anaemia or other underlying causes. Depending on findings, the GP may refer to a specialist or arrange further tests. The goal is to identify any treatable cause while considering how much the bleeding is affecting the person.
