Privacy

Private by default, plain about limits.

recHERsion is a screening and awareness support tool, not a diagnosis. Here is what stays in your browser, what is stored locally, and what leaves this device.

Browser

Anonymous session

A random anonymous ID is kept in a browser cookie so your journal and summary stay together. It is not your name, email or phone number.

An optional display name is stored only in a cookie on this device. It is not written to the SQLite database.

Local database

Saved check-ins

Check-ins that the app decides to save are stored in a local SQLite database. Distress messages are not written to the saved-entry database.

You can download a JSON copy of your saved entries from the journal, or clear them from the journal and chat page.

Your music

Sound uploads

Audio files you add on the sound page stay in this browser using private local storage. They are not uploaded to recHERsion.

Removing a track deletes it from this browser. Clearing site data also removes your uploaded tracks.

Network

Maps and AI

Map searches open in Google Maps. Your location is requested only when you press the location button, and it is not sent to recHERsion.

If an LLM key is configured, de-identified message text may be sent to that provider. Without a key, the app uses rule-based fallbacks.

Local logs

Debug trace

The local agent trace can contain short input and output summaries to explain how an answer was produced. It stays on this machine and is not shown to other users.

Do not run a shared deployment without reviewing trace retention and access.

Limits

Not a medical record

This app does not diagnose, prescribe or replace a clinician. It cites the knowledge base it uses and encourages you to talk to a doctor about anything that worries you.
